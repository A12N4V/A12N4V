Undergraduate at **UC Irvine**, working where **medicine** meets **computation**: biodigital twins, medical AI, and the cybernetics that ties them together. I build **[Homonin](https://homonin.com)**, a cyber-medical research workspace.

A body produces **signals**. A model turns those signals into **signs**. A controller acts on the signs, and the body answers. I work on that loop.

**FOCUS** &nbsp; biodigital twins · medical AI · neural systems · cybernetics
